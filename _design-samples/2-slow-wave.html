01 Research

Timing sound to the sleeping brain

I'm a Ph.D. student in EECS at MIT, working with Dr. Laura Lewis. My goal is to study brain physiology with computational modeling and build functional neuromodulation systems with engineering tools. Before MIT, I worked on neural decoding, medical devices, and signal processing at Pitt, CMU, and UC San Diego.

01 · sense

Scalp EEG

Record brain activity during sleep, even inside the MRI scanner.

02 · predict

Real-time phase

Denoise the signal and predict slow-oscillation phase with machine learning.

03 · stimulate

Sound at the peak

Play a brief burst of sound aligned with the slow-wave up-phase.

04 · measure

CSF flow

Image cerebrospinal fluid with simultaneous fMRI to measure the effect.
