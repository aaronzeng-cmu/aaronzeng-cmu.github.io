Xiangjian Zeng (Aaron)

I build closed-loop auditory stimulation systems that use real-time EEG to strengthen cerebrospinal fluid flow during sleep.

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at MIT, working with Dr. Laura Lewis. My systems use machine-learning slow-oscillation phase prediction to time sound to the sleeping brain. I'm a co-author of our 2026 study in Science Translational Medicine.

My goal is to study brain physiology with computational modeling and build functional neuromodulation systems with engineering tools. Before MIT, I worked on computational neuroscience, neural decoding, medical devices, and signal processing in labs at Pitt, CMU, and UC San Diego.
