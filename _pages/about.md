I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at MIT, working with Dr. Laura Lewis in the Lewis Lab. My systems use machine-learning slow-oscillation phase prediction to time sound to the sleeping brain. I am a co-author of [our 2026 study in _Science Translational Medicine_](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aea5469).

My research objective is to study brain physiology with computational modeling and build functional neuromodulation systems with engineering toolsets. Before MIT, I worked on computational neuroscience, neural decoding, medical devices, and signal processing in labs at Pitt, CMU, and UC San Diego.
